Use of the data

UNCTAD is responsible for the data in this consultation, carried out with the WFZO Regional Office for Central Asia. Your name, position, entity, email, answers and any documents you provide are used for the needs assessment, for follow-up with participants and for preparing the findings. Your personal link lets you come back and correct your answers.

The data are kept for 12 months after the consultation closes. Answers are read only by UNCTAD and the WFZO Regional Office for Central Asia. Findings are shared with the responsible authorities first and are published only in consolidated regional form. To consult, correct or withdraw your data, write to nelson.perez@un.org. See also the UNCTAD privacy notice.
